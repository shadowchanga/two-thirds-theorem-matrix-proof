# The Two-Thirds Theorem: A Matrix Proof

This paper gives a clearer route through Claude/Anthropic's proof that at least two thirds of the nontrivial zeros of the Riemann zeta function are simple and lie on the critical line, and at least five sixths are distinct. The main idea is a finite Hermitian matrix whose positive and negative eigenvalues control the zero counts.
